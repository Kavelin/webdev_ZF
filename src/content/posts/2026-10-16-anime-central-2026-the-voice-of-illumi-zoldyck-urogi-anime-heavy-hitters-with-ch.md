## Anime Central 2026: The Voice of Illumi Zoldyck & Urogi: Anime Heavy-Hitters with Chris Hackney

Step into the booth with Chris Hackney, the versatile American voice actor behind some of gaming and anime’s most beloved and complex characters! Whether he’s commanding the battlefield as Dimitri Alexandre Blaiddyd in Fire Emblem: Three Houses, guiding Link as the noble Rauru in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, or slicing through enemies as Kamisato Ayato in Genshin Impact, Chris brings a deeply compelling presence to every role. His anime resume is just as stacked, lending his voice to the chilling Illumi Zoldyck in Hunter x Hunter, Urogi in Demon Slayer, Khun Aguero Agnes in Tower of God, Athrun Zala in Gundam SEED, and Ghiaccio in JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Wind. From fierce mecha pilots like V.IV Rusty in Armored Core VI to calculated strategists, Chris Hackney is a powerhouse of character-driven performances.About Our Guest:Chris Hackney is a Los Angeles-based American voice actor renowned for his dynamic, character-driven performances across video games and anime. Originally from Florida, his background in music eventually led him to discover his true passion in acting and voiceover. In the gaming sphere, Chris is best known as Dimitri Alexandre Blaiddyd in Fire Emblem: Three Houses, Kamisato Ayato in Genshin Impact, Rauru in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, and V.IV Rusty in Armored Core VI: Fires of Rubicon. His extensive anime credits include fan-favorite roles such as Illumi Zoldyck in Hunter x Hunter, Urogi in Demon Slayer, Khun Aguero Agnes in Tower of God, Athrun Zala in the Mobile Suit Gundam SEED franchise, and Ghiaccio in JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.🛒 Fuel Your Fandom:Looking to upgrade your collection?
