## FREEDY VS JASON | Love Jinx (Geonhan) Chapter #57

📖 ABOUT Lim Haegyung has a strange jinx. Whenever he is kissed or kisses someone, something bad follows. All the boyfriends involved with him in a kiss either break up with him or he ends up getting caught in some other mess. He meets Jung Wook, who goes in the same office as him, and they become lovers. Even though Jung Wook understands Haegyung's situation, Haegyung ends up initiating a kiss accidentally. Because of his jinx, Haegyung ends up in a whole new chaotic situation wherein he meets the guy responsible for his jinx. Lim Haegyung blames Kang Woojin, a guy from his past, for his jinx. Broke and helpless, Haegyung is offered a place in the same apartment as him. As the story unfolds, the past between them is revealed.
