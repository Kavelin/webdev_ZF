## BRIAN BEACOCK Interview - Digimon, Bungou Stray Dogs, Bleach & Beyond! | Anime Magic 2023

About @BrianBeacockDive into the dynamic world of voice actor Kaiji Tang! 🎙️ Known for his iconic roles as Gojo in Jujutsu Kaisen, Guts in Berserk, Osamu Dazai in Bungou Stray Dogs, Hendrickson in Seven Deadly Sins, Jin Kazama in Tekken, Sanemi Shinazugawa in Demon Slayer, Amidamaru in Shaman King, Archer in the Fate Series and many more, Kaiji Tang's versatile talent has left an indelible mark on the world of anime and gaming. Join us for an exclusive glimpse into his career, characters, and the magic of bringing these beloved figures to life on the screen!
